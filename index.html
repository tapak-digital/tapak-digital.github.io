Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A small business landing page usually takes 3 to 5 working days, a company profile 1 to 2 weeks, and an online store/custom build depends on the features. I will give you an exact timeline in the proposal.

I don't have a logo, photos, or copy yet. Can you help?

Yes. I can help write the basic copy and use licensed photos/illustrations. A logo and professional product photos can be discussed as add-ons.

Can I update the website content myself?

From the Company Profile package up, you get a dashboard to edit text, images, and articles without any coding, along with a guide on how to use it.

How does payment work?

50% deposit up front, and the remaining 50% once the website is finished and you approve the result, before full access is handed over.

What do I pay after the first year?

Only the yearly domain and hosting renewal. I tell you the cost up front so there are no surprises.

Will my website show up on Google?

I register your website with Google Search Console and build it with basic SEO. Search ranking takes time and depends on competition, so I don't promise any specific position.
